## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-13

### Added

- Local CSV profiling with reviewable SDV metadata.
- Gaussian Copula and CTGAN candidate generation with immutable manifests.
- Statistical-fidelity, exact-copy, near-neighbor, DCR, and optional ML-utility evidence.
- Deterministic threshold decisions, candidate ranking, and winner selection.
- Streamlit audit studio with a built-in demo and artifact downloads.
- Five-tool FastMCP stdio interface with resolved-path allowlisting.
- Automated tests, static checks, package verification, threat model, and CI.
